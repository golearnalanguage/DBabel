# Code of conduct

DBabel welcomes technically rigorous contributions and disagreement. Participation in the repository should remain professional and focused on reproducible evidence, documented behavior, and the project method.

Contributors should:

- discuss people and organizations respectfully;
- critique evidence, rules, implementations, and technical claims rather than individuals;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, or disclosure of private information;
- avoid posting confidential or copyrighted material that cannot be redistributed;
- disclose relevant uncertainty and source limitations when proposing terminology-method changes.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that materially violate these rules and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its contributors.
