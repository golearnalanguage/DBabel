# Terminology classification

## GENERIC_DB
Concepts used broadly across database systems. Generic does not mean every product defines the concept identically.

## STANDARD_SQL
Terms whose meaning/designation is materially governed by an applicable SQL/data standard.

## VENDOR_CONCEPT
A technical concept whose official naming/meaning is tied to a vendor or product family.

## PRODUCT_NAME
Official product, feature, component, service, edition, or branded name. Protect by default.

## UI_LABEL
Exact GUI/CLI visible label. Prefer same-version official UI evidence.

## SQL_PARAM_IDENTIFIER
Executable syntax, configuration key, variable, identifier, option, or parameter. Protect by default.

## COMMAND_PATH_FILENAME
Command, path, executable, filename, extension, switch. Protect by default.

## ACRONYM
Treat full form, abbreviation, casing, first-use policy, and scope separately.

## AMBIGUOUS_HIGH_RISK
Terms whose meaning changes materially by product or context, such as generic words that can denote different database objects/roles. These require context before normalization.
