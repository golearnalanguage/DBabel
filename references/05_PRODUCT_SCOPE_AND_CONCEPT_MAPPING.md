# Product scope and concept mapping

## Scope resolution
Use the narrowest reliable scope. A comparison table may contain one vendor per column; a sentence may mention multiple products.

## Mapping rules
Cross-vendor concepts must not be treated as synonyms merely because they serve similar purposes.

Allowed relation labels:
- `synonym_of`
- `related_to`
- `product_implementation_of`
- `roughly_corresponds_to`
- `broader_than`
- `narrower_than`
- `not_equivalent_to`

Every mapping produced by the Agent is task-specific evidence-backed analysis unless supplied by the user. DBabel does not ship a canonical cross-vendor mapping table.
