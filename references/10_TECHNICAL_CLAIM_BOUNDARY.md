# Technical claim verification

Terminology correctness does not prove factual correctness.

Route to `OUT_OF_SCOPE_CLAIM` unless claim verification is explicitly requested:
- performance/benchmark numbers;
- compatibility percentages;
- product capability assertions;
- licensing/support statements;
- version availability/support lifecycle;
- security/compliance certifications;
- superiority/comparative claims;
- quantitative limits unless the terminology task specifically requires them.

The Agent may still normalize terminology inside such a claim while clearly stating that the claim itself was not validated.
